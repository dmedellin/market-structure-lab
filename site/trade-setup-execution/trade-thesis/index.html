Core concepts

Use these concepts as explicit rules rather than changing them after seeing the outcome.

C

Context

State the higher-timeframe structure and the location that makes the setup relevant.

T

Trigger

Define the observable event that permits entry. Directional bias alone is not a trigger.

I

Invalidation

Specify the price or condition that proves the thesis wrong before position size is calculated.

Trade thesis process

A thesis is complete only when every decision can be evaluated objectively after the trade.

1

Describe context

Identify structure, location, and any higher-timeframe conflict.

2

Name the setup

Use one defined setup such as pullback, breakout, or reversal.

3

Require a trigger

Write the exact close, retest, or structure event required for entry.

4

Define failure and objective

Set structural invalidation, target, and the no-trade condition.

Minimum thesis template

1
ContextState the higher-timeframe structure and current location.
2
ConditionDescribe what price must do before an entry is allowed.
3
InvalidationUse the price that disproves the thesis—not the amount you hope to lose.
4
ObjectiveChoose the next opposing structural level and verify sufficient reward.

Common mistakes

1
Bias presented as a trade plan“Bullish” or “bearish” does not specify when to enter or when the idea is wrong.
2
Invalidation selected from desired dollar lossDetermine structural invalidation first, then reduce position size to fit the risk budget.
3
Target selected only to force 2RA mathematical target is not useful when structural resistance or support appears first.
4
Changing conditions after entryA thesis that can be rewritten after every candle cannot be tested.
