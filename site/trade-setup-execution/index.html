The course

What this course is

One trade, taken apart and taught in the order you would actually do it. Each lesson is a page you click through rather than a chapter you read.

15 lessons · 5 stages

What it teaches

The mechanics of a single trade: a conditional plan (01), the levels it references (02), and the independent evidence behind it (03); the three setups that keep recurring — breakout, pullback, reversal (04–06); the trigger candle (07), the stop (08), and the targets (09); reward-to-risk arithmetic (10), position size (11), and candle-by-candle management (12); then backtesting (13), a structured journal (14), and the review that closes the loop (15).

Who it is for

Anyone who can already read a chart and now wants a repeatable procedure for acting on one. Course 1 is the assumed background: this course places stops at structural levels and enters on pullbacks without stopping to define either. It also suits someone who trades already and cannot say, from records rather than memory, which of their setups actually pays.

How to work through it

In order, 01 to 15, one stage at a time. Each stage needs the one before it: you cannot confirm an entry (07) before you can name the setup (04–06), sizing (11) needs the stop distance from 08, and the review in 15 has nothing to read until 14 has recorded something. One lesson per sitting is plenty. The last stage is what makes the first fourteen stick.

Interop

Lessons 14 and 15 share one file format

The journal and the review are two halves of one workflow, so the record written by the first is the input to the second — no retyping, and no third-party service in between.

Format: trade-journal-v1

How the handoff works

Lesson 14 keeps your entries in your own browser's local storage and exports them as a JSON document stamped trade-journal-v1. Lesson 15 picks the same stored journal up automatically when it is there, and will also read an exported trade-journal-v1 file you choose from disk — so a journal kept on one machine can be reviewed on another. Both pages run entirely in the browser: nothing is uploaded, and there is no account anywhere in this course.

The schema linked beside this is the contract between them. It is worth reading if you would rather keep the journal in your own spreadsheet or script and only bring it here for the review: match the shape and lesson 15 will read it.
