Real data, and what that does and does not mean

This page analyses real market data on a real security. IREN is a listed company, and every bar, price, indicator and backtest result comes from the market data cited in the sources at the foot of this page. It is educational analysis, published for educational use only: it is not personalized investment advice, not a trade signal, and not a guarantee of any outcome.

The backtests here are exploratory

  • The patterns were chosen after inspecting this sample, not before it.
  • Every signal is evaluated at the session close, and every forward result is a future close-to-close return.
  • Transaction costs and execution effects are excluded.
  • The samples are small — several studies rest on fewer than ten events.
  • None of it is an out-of-sample forecast. The studies describe what happened inside one 50-session window and nothing else.

Every figure on this page derives from one published file: iren-analysis-data.json — the 50-session dataset, its derived weekly bars, indicators, pattern studies, analog states, levels and scenarios. The arithmetic is yours to check.

Learning objectives

What this lab does

The goal is to describe the market and define conditions—not to pretend one historical sample can predict one future day.

02

Measure what happened after large moves, gaps, momentum runs and weak closes in the available 50-session sample.

03

Build tomorrow scenarios

Use support, resistance, volatility, analog states and catalysts to define bullish, range and bearish paths.
