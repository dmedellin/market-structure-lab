Backtest comparison

One-day pattern outcomes

Most relevant behavior: large upside moves tended to cool; the two valid three-up sequences also cooled, while large selloffs often rebounded. These results describe a highly mean-reverting, volatile sample.
Two valid events — descriptive only. The three-consecutive-up-closes study requires three complete positive close-to-close returns, so the incomplete rolling windows at the start of the sample are excluded. That leaves two valid events in the 50-session sample. The result is descriptive only and is not statistically reliable: do not read a direction into two observations.
