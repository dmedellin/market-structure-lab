Learning path

The path, in order

Two courses, 22 lessons in total, meant to be taken front to back. Course 1 is the prerequisite: it teaches the chart-reading vocabulary — structure, ranges, timeframes, invalidation — that Course 2 assumes you already have. Course 2 then turns that read into an executable plan. Open a course to see its lessons and start anywhere inside it.

Educational use only — nothing here is trading advice. Every chart, price, quote, trade, fill, and performance figure on this site is a synthetic example generated to illustrate a concept. No lesson describes a real instrument or a real market session, and nothing on these pages is a recommendation, a signal, or a forecast. Trading carries the risk of losing money, and real outcomes can differ from any illustration here because of spread, slippage, commissions, gaps, taxes, liquidity, assignment, exercise, implied volatility, time decay, and other factors.

    Step 2 · Plan and execute a trade

    Execution & risk ~5 hr 45 min in total

    Trade Setup and Execution

    Plan and execute a trade — entry, stop, target, size, and review.

    Prerequisite: Course 1. This course assumes you can already find structure, ranges, and invalidation on a bare chart. It does not re-teach chart reading; it starts from the plan you build on top of it.

    Fifteen lessons that turn a read into an executable plan: write a conditional thesis, mark the zones that matter, and weigh confluence before working through breakout, pullback, and reversal setups. From there it is mechanics — entry confirmation, stop placement, targets, reward-to-risk, position sizing, and managing a trade you are already in — closing with backtesting, a structured journal, and the performance review that tells you what to fix next.
