Seven labs that teach the chart.

A complete, ordered course on how price actually moves — from reading structure on a bare chart, through breakouts, context, entries and risk, to picking an options contract. Every lab is one self-contained interactive page: no accounts, no tracking, nothing to install. Take them in order; each one leans on the one before it.

Curriculum

The course, in order

Seven labs, each a single self-contained page of interactive charts, worked explanations, and a way to test yourself. They build on one another: read structure first, then where it breaks, then how to enter it, risk it, and express it.

7 labs · start at 01

Educational use only — nothing here is trading advice. Every chart, price, quote, and options figure in this course is a synthetic example generated to illustrate a concept. No lab describes a real instrument or a real market session, and nothing on these pages is a recommendation, a signal, or a forecast. Trading carries the risk of losing money.
