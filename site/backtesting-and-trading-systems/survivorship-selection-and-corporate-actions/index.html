Survivorship, Selection, and Corporate Actions

Build a point-in-time universe, include failed and delisted instruments, and adjust splits and distributions without using information learned later.

Review concepts
Market behavior · Universe integrityΣ

A historical universe must contain what was actually available at each point in time.

Keeping only today’s survivors removes failures. Selecting assets after seeing returns makes the test know the future.

Practical rules

  • Use point-in-time constituent membership.
  • Include delisting returns or a documented conservative treatment.
  • Verify whether vendor prices are raw or adjusted.

Common mistakes

  • Downloading today’s index members for a ten-year test.
  • Removing bankrupt symbols as bad data.
  • Mixing adjusted prices with unadjusted cash flows.
