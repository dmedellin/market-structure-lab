Lesson 03 · Context

Multi-timeframe market structure

The higher timeframe tells you the environment. The lower timeframe tells you whether the pullback is still unfolding or has produced a usable trigger. Context above; execution below.

Use the four-step workflow
One chart can contain another trend

The hierarchy

Timeframes answer different questions. Mixing their jobs creates contradictory signals.

W

Regime

Weekly and daily structure reveal the larger trend, major range, and important protected swings.

D

Location

The intermediate chart shows whether price is at support, resistance, a range edge, or an unhelpful midpoint.

H

Trigger

The lower timeframe shows the pullback, reaction, structure shift, and precise invalidation for execution.

Top-down conclusion

The daily trend is bullish, price is pulling back into support, and the 1-hour chart has shifted back upward.

Decision Aligned bullish setup

The lower-timeframe trigger agrees with the higher-timeframe direction at a useful location.

What this is normally read as

On the daily, read as buyers still stepping in before the previous low is reached: the pullback's lowest close held above the marked 110.2. On the hourly the same pullback stopped making lower lows and then took out its last lower high — the identical reading one scale down. A daily close below 110.2 breaks both at once.

Important: “Lower timeframe bearish” does not automatically mean “short.” First ask whether that bearish move is simply the pullback of a bullish higher timeframe.

The four-step workflow

Read from large to small, then define the invalidation at the execution timeframe.

1

Regime

Is the higher timeframe bullish, bearish, or ranging? Mark the protected swing.

2

Location

Is price at a meaningful edge, prior breakout, protected swing, or merely the middle?

3

Trigger

Has the lower timeframe actually changed character in the intended direction?

4

Invalidation

Which lower-timeframe swing proves the trigger failed without contradicting the higher-timeframe thesis?
