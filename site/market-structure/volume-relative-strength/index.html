Lesson 06 · Participation

Volume & relative strength

Price shows direction. Volume helps you judge participation. Relative strength asks whether the asset is actually leading or lagging its benchmark—even when both charts are green or both are falling.

Compare against a benchmark
Compare volume to its own baseline

What each lens contributes

Neither volume nor relative strength replaces structure. They add evidence about participation and leadership.

V

Volume context

Compare current activity with the asset’s recent baseline. A large bar matters more when it is unusual for that market.

✓

Follow-through

High volume can accompany continuation or exhaustion. The next candles reveal whether the price move was accepted.

RS

Relative strength

Normalize the asset and benchmark, then compare performance. This is not the RSI momentum indicator.

Price + volume laboratory

Inspect the event bar, its volume relative to baseline, and what price does afterward.

Participation diagnosis

Price closes above the range on expanded volume and remains accepted outside.

Interpretation Breakout supported

Participation expanded during the break and follow-through held above the old range.

What this is normally read as

The bar that closed above 108 traded 2,240 against a baseline near 950, and no bar after it fell back to that baseline. Read conventionally, activity expanded at the new prices instead of drying up, which is what separates a break that holds from one that fades. A close back inside 108 would break the reading.

A practical confirmation stack

1
Structure firstDefine the trend, range boundary, breakout, or protected swing before interpreting volume.
2
Participation secondCompare event volume with recent activity and note whether up or down candles attract more participation.
3
Leadership thirdPrefer assets whose performance is improving against the benchmark when that agrees with your directional thesis.
4
Follow-through decidesA breakout with impressive volume can still fail; acceptance after the event remains essential.
