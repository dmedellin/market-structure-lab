Lesson 02 · Boundaries

Ranges, breakouts & liquidity sweeps

Learn to separate a real break from a dramatic wick. The central question is not “Did price cross the line?” but “Did price close beyond it, hold there, and build structure?”

Open the lab See the decision framework
Wick ≠ confirmed break

Three ideas to master

A range is a negotiation zone. Its boundaries become meaningful because traders repeatedly react there.

↔

Range

Price rotates between a recognizable high and low. The middle usually offers less information than the edges.

✓

Acceptance

Price closes beyond a boundary, spends time outside, and either holds or forms new structure there.

↶

Liquidity sweep

Price trades beyond an obvious level, then rejects and closes back inside. The failed break is the evidence.

Working rule: Treat a wick beyond the boundary with a close back inside as a sweep candidate. Treat a strong close outside as evidence—not final proof—of a breakout.

The boundary decision framework

Classify the sequence, not a single candle.

1

Rejection

Price reaches a boundary and rotates away without materially trading through it.

2

Confirmed breakout

A close lands outside the range and subsequent candles remain accepted beyond the boundary.

3

Breakout + retest

Price exits, returns to the old boundary, holds it from the other side, and resumes.

4

Liquidity sweep

Price pierces the level, fails to remain outside, and often accelerates back through the range.
