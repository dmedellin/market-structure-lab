Lesson 05 · Risk

Invalidation, stops & reward-to-risk

A stop should express where the trade idea is wrong—not where the loss becomes emotionally uncomfortable. Position size adapts to the structural stop; the stop should not be squeezed to justify a larger position.

Practice stop placement
Risk first; size second

The risk chain

Every number should derive from the thesis rather than from the profit you hope to make.

X

Invalidation

The price event that disproves the setup: a protected swing breaks, a breakout fails, or acceptance returns inside a range.

#

Position size

Risk budget divided by risk per unit. Wider structural stops require fewer units; tighter valid stops allow more.

R

R multiple

Potential reward divided by planned risk. It compares geometry, not the probability that the target will be reached.

Three different stop problems

A
Inside noiseThe stop can be hit while the original structure remains valid. Position size looks larger, but the stop does not express the thesis.
B
Beyond invalidationThe stop sits past the protected swing with a deliberate buffer. The position is sized around this real distance.
C
Arbitrarily farA stop can be structurally unnecessary as well as too tight. Extra distance reduces size without adding a defined reason.
R
A 3R target is not automatically superior to a 1.5R target.

R describes payoff geometry. Setup quality, target realism, liquidity, gaps, and execution still matter.
