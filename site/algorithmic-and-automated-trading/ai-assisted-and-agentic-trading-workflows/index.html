AI-Assisted and Agentic Trading Workflows

Use AI for research, extraction, classification, and trade proposals while keeping market data, rules, risk, permissions, and execution controls deterministic and auditable.

Review concepts
Market behavior · AI decision boundaryAI

AI can propose an interpretation, but observable market data and fixed controls determine whether an action is allowed.

A model may describe buyer control, but the system must verify actual higher prices, volume, freshness, risk, and invalidation before creating an order.

Practical rules

  • Use AI for advisory work before execution authority.
  • Require structured outputs with evidence and timestamps.
  • Validate every proposal with deterministic rules and risk controls.

Common mistakes

  • Allowing free-form model output to call the broker directly.
  • Treating confidence as probability of profit.
  • Using stale news or market context without validation.
